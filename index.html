    Coach etiquette

    Keeping it clean

    • Your driver passes a trash bag back through the coach. Add yours and hand it to the row behind you.
    • Spilled something? Tell the driver at the next stop.
    • Keep the aisle clear of bags, feet and blankets.

    While we're moving

    • Stay seated when you can. If your seat has a belt, wear it.
    • Headphones for music and video. Keep calls short and quiet.
    • In an emergency, follow your driver. Exits are marked on windows and roof hatches.

    Luggage & policies

    Florida Line Run

    What's included
    • Two checked bags beneath the coach, plus one small carry-on that fits overhead or under your seat.
    • Suitcases up to 55 lbs, about 27" x 21" x 14". Standard luggage tops out at 31".
    • Your own items only. Items for people not riding pay freight charges.
    Extra, oversized or heavy items
    • Extra pieces travel as space allows. The fee is set by size and weight and paid to the driver on the day of travel.
    • Coolers, tubs, chairs, strollers and similar count as more than one piece.
    • Anything over 55 lbs is an additional $30, determined by weight.
    • Golf clubs, bicycles and bike trailers are freight. Freight can't be booked ahead, and is only picked up and dropped off at passenger stops.
    • No bicycles in busy season, roughly November through April.
    Not permitted
    • E-bike lithium batteries. U.S. safety law doesn't allow us to carry them.
    Changes & cancellations
    • Cancel at least 7 days before departure for a credit or refund. Card refunds are less a 3% processing fee.
    • Any change to a reservation (dates, names, boarding or drop-off stop) carries a $20 service charge.
